Top Tens vs. Rankly: What’s the Difference?

By Top Tens

The short answer

Both apps make top 10 lists, and both are free to start. The difference is what happens after the list is made. Rankly leans into discovery: it matches you with people who share your taste, turns disagreements into vote brackets, and maps the best-ranked spots in your city. Top Tens leans into the debate itself: compare two lists head-to-head, fuse several friends’ lists into one Master ranking, and challenge friends directly — and it does it without advertising profiling or selling your personal information.

What Rankly does

Rankly (for iPhone and Android) is a top 10 list maker: pick a category, add up to ten things, put them in order. Its signature features are:

  • TasteBuds — scores how much your taste overlaps with other people, based only on the things you both ranked, and surfaces the people who match.
  • Rank-offs — turn a disagreement into a bracket your friends vote on.
  • Hotspots — a map of the best-ranked restaurants, bars, and cafés in your city, built from everyone’s lists.
  • Spotify import — drop in a playlist URL and Rankly pulls the tracks, album art, and links automatically.

Rankly is free with up to five lists. Rankly+ costs €1.99 a month or €9.99 a year (with a 7-day free trial) and adds unlimited lists, no ads, and the full Hotspots map.

What Top Tens does

Top Tens is also free to download. Its optional paid subscription is Premium at $0.99/month, or Premium + Storage at $2.99/month (prices as shown in the app, November 2026). Its signature features are:

  • Compare & fuse — put two lists head-to-head, or fuse lists from as many friends as you like into one definitive Master ranking that settles the debate.
  • Challenges — send challenge links to friends and see whose taste reigns supreme.
  • Shareable cards — turn any list into a visual card you can share and post anywhere.
  • Reference links — every item can link out to Wikipedia and Amazon, so lists stay useful as well as fun.
  • Privacy by policy — no advertising profiling, no selling your personal information, no marketing emails. Your lists are yours.

Which one fits you?

  • Pick Rankly if you mainly want to discover people and places: find your taste matches, browse a map of ranked spots, and import Spotify playlists.
  • Pick Top Tens if you mainly want to settle debates with your own circle: fuse your friends’ lists into one Master ranking, challenge each other head-to-head, and keep your rankings out of the advertising machine.
  • Try both. Both are free to start, and a list made in one costs nothing to remake in the other. The right answer is whichever one your friends are actually on.

As listed on Rankly’s own site when this post was written (November 2026): free for up to five lists; Rankly+ €1.99/month or €9.99/year with a 7-day free trial. Prices can change — check their site for the current figure.

Are the Top Tens prices right?
As shown in the app when this post was written (November 2026): Premium $0.99/month, Premium + Storage $2.99/month. Prices can change — check the app for the current figure.

Does Top Tens have ads?
What Top Tens states in its privacy policy is about data: no advertising profiling, no selling your personal information, and no marketing emails. That is the tracking question, and the answer is no.

Can I use Top Tens without an account?
You can browse the pre-seeded stock vault without one. Your own vault needs an account — email plus a one-time code, no password.

The bottom line

There is no wrong answer here, only a different emphasis. Rankly helps you find people and places that match your taste. Top Tens helps you prove your taste to the people you already know — and keeps your rankings yours while you do it.
