What Can I Rank on Top Tens?

By Top Tens

The short answer

Anything. Movies, music, restaurants, sports teams, books, video games, snacks, travel spots — if you can rank it, you can Top Tens it. Your personal vault is designed for lists across any categories you can imagine, not a fixed menu of approved topics.

Ideas to start with

  • Movies: top 10 films of all time, best opening scenes, best movie villains, films to watch on a rainy Sunday.
  • Music: top 10 albums of the year, best songs to drive to, best concert openers, most underrated bands.
  • Food: best pizza places near you, top 10 snacks, best coffee shops, desserts worth the trip.
  • Sports: greatest players at your position, best teams of the decade, best sports movies, best stadiums you’ve been to.
  • Books and TV: top 10 novels, best TV finales, books that should be movies, shows to binge in a weekend.
  • Games: best video games of all time, hardest bosses, best soundtracks, games to replay forever.
  • Everyday life: best sandwiches, top 10 chores to avoid, best road-trip stops, worst things to step on barefoot.

What makes a list worth making?

  • Pick something you actually care about. A list of ten things you love beats a list of ten things you think you should love.
  • Narrow it down. “Top 10 movies” is a fine start, but “top 10 movies to watch on a rainy Sunday” is a list people argue about — and arguing is the point.
  • Leave room to disagree. The best lists are the ones your friends immediately want to fix. Share it as a visual card, send a challenge link, or fuse everyone’s lists into one Master ranking and let the debate settle it.
  • Use the extras. Add reference links and thumbnail media to each item, or let the app add them — every item can link out to Wikipedia and Amazon, so your list is useful as well as fun.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on how many lists I can make?
Your vault holds lists across any categories you dream up. The optional paid subscription adds more vault space; the free tier covers getting started and regular use.

Can I rank something unusual?
Yes — there is no approved list of categories. If you can rank it, it belongs in a list.

What if my friends rank the same thing differently?
That is the entire idea. Compare the two lists head-to-head, or fuse them with other friends’ lists into one Master ranking and see whose taste wins.

The bottom line

The only wrong category is the one you don’t care about. Start with the argument you’ve already had a hundred times — the best film, the best pizza, the best album — and put it in writing. Then send it to the person who disagrees most.
