How Do I Make a Top 10 List?

By Top Tens

The short answer

Pick a category, add ten things, put them in order. That is the whole job — and Top Tens is built around those three steps, with the arguing afterwards built in.

Step by step

  1. Pick a category. Anything you can rank: movies, albums, restaurants, sports teams, books, video games, snacks, travel spots. Your lists live in your personal vault, organized by category.
  2. Add ten things. Add the items you want to rank. Each item can carry reference links and thumbnail media, or you can let the app add them for you — every item can link out to Wikipedia and Amazon, so a list doubles as something useful, not just an opinion.
  3. Put them in order. Add, edit, delete, and reorder items with a tap until the order feels right. Your #1 is your #1 — there is no algorithm re-sorting it for you.
  4. Save it to your vault. Your own vault needs an account, which is passwordless: an email address and a one-time verification code, no password to remember. You can browse the pre-seeded stock vault without an account if you just want to look around first.

Then what? A list is only the start

  • Compare it. Put your list head-to-head against a friend’s list on the same category and see where you agree and where you don’t.
  • Fuse it. Combine lists from several friends into one Master ranking — the definitive answer, at least until someone changes their mind.
  • Challenge someone. Send a challenge link and see whose taste wins. Or share the list as a visual card, postable anywhere.
  • Change your mind. Reorder any time. A list that can’t change is a list that goes stale the moment you discover something better.

Tips for a good list

  • Be specific. “Top 10 movies” is a fine start, but “top 10 movies to watch on a rainy Sunday” is a list people actually argue about.
  • Rank what you know. A list is only as honest as the items on it. Leave the slot empty rather than ranking something you haven’t tried.
  • Expect disagreement. That is the point. A list nobody argues with is a list nobody cared about.

Frequently asked questions

Does it have to be exactly ten items?
It is a top ten list — ten is the format. The fun is in the constraint: something has to miss the cut, and that is where the arguments start.

Can I make a list without an account?
You can browse the stock vault without one, but creating and keeping your own lists needs an account — email plus a one-time code, no password.

What if I change my mind later?
Edit, delete, and reorder with a tap. Lists are yours, and they stay editable.

The bottom line

Making a top 10 list takes about a minute. Defending it takes considerably longer — and that is exactly why it is worth doing.
