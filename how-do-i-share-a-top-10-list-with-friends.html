How Do I Share a Top 10 List with Friends?

By Top Tens

The short answer

Three ways, depending on what you want to happen next. Share the list as a visual card anyone can see, send a challenge link to a friend, or compare your list against theirs head-to-head. All three are free, and none of them needs the other person to already have the app just to look.

1. Share it as a visual card

Top Tens turns your list into a shareable visual card — the whole top 10, laid out to look good — that you can share and post anywhere: a group chat, social media, a forum, a text message. This is the way to show a list off. It is also the way lists travel: someone sees your card, disagrees with your #4, and the argument starts without anyone opening the app.

2. Send a challenge link

Want a verdict, not just an audience? Send a challenge link to a Top Tens friend and see whose taste reigns supreme. A challenge is a direct contest over the same category — your list against theirs — and it is the fastest way to turn a quiet opinion into a running rivalry.

3. Compare — and fuse — head-to-head

Put two lists on the same category side by side and see exactly where you agree and where you part ways. And if more friends pile in, fuse all of those lists into one Master ranking: a single definitive list built from everyone’s taste, settling the debate once and for all — at least until someone changes their mind and reopens it.

What your friends need

To see a shared card or a challenge link, they just open the link. To make their own lists, compare, and challenge you back, they need the app — free on Google Play — and an account, which is passwordless: an email address and a one-time verification code, no password to remember.

Frequently asked questions

Can I share a list outside the app?
Yes. Shared visual cards are designed to be postable anywhere, not just inside Top Tens.

Do shared lists expose my data?
What you share is the list itself — the rankings you chose to share. Top Tens does not sell your personal information or use your data for advertising profiling, and shared cards only carry what you put in the list.

Can I change a list after sharing it?
Your lists stay editable — add, edit, delete, and reorder with a tap — so a shared list can be updated when your taste changes. (A card already posted elsewhere shows the version as it was when it was shared.)

The bottom line

A list kept to yourself is a diary entry. Share it as a card, challenge a friend, or fuse it into a Master ranking — that is when a top 10 list does what it is for.
